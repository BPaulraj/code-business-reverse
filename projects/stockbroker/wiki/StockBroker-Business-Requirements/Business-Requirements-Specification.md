## 2. Executive summary

StockBroker is a **simulated retail stock-broking platform**. A client:
1. registers
2. funds a cash wallet by (simulated) bank transfer or debit card
3. buys and sells whole shares in 41 listed US companies at simulated, slightly fluctuating prices
4. receives a PDF invoice and inbox notifications for every trade

No real money, market data or payment network is involved, and the product says so on every money-related screen and document.

The platform is reached through two **channels**: a self-service **web portal**, and a **public REST API** for external client applications. The API offers most of the portal's operations, but not all of them.

**Headline risks** (section 11):
- A failure after a trade has already gone through can show the client an error, which invites a duplicate trade.
- Only the portal's button prevents duplicate orders. The platform itself doesn't detect them, and API callers have no protection at all.
- The trading rules are maintained twice, once per channel.

**Key open decisions** (section 12):
- price tolerance between the quote and the execution price
- whether the illustrative brokerage fee should be charged
- the KYC process
- who the public API is for

## 7. Business processes

### P-001 — Place a trade

**Goal:** the client buys or sells whole shares. Cash and shares move immediately, and the trade is documented.
**Trigger:** the client confirms an order in the portal, or an external application submits one through the API.

1. The client picks a company and chooses Buy or Sell. The screen shows the quoted price.
2. The client enters a quantity and reviews the order. The portal checks the quantity, the cash (at the quoted price) and the shares held.
3. The client confirms. The confirm button is disabled until the result arrives.
4. The platform takes a **new** price at that moment. This is the execution price.
5. **Buy:** the platform checks the cash, takes the total from the wallet and adds the shares to the holding at a weighted average cost.
   **Sell:** the platform checks the shares held, reduces or removes the holding and adds the total to the wallet.
6. The trade is recorded as Completed with an invoice. All money and share changes happen together, or not at all.
7. The client receives a "Trade executed" message and an invoice message.

**Outcomes when the order doesn't go through**

| Situation | What the client sees | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Invalid quantity, or not enough cash or shares at review | Message on screen | Nothing sent |
| Not enough cash at the execution price | "Insufficient wallet balance for this order" | Nothing changes |
| Not enough shares (e.g. sold elsewhere meanwhile) | "Insufficient holdings for this order" | Nothing changes |
| Platform busy or rare internal error | "Internal server error" | Nothing changes |
| **Notification fails after the trade completed** | **"Internal server error"** | **The trade went through.** A retry would trade again (section 11). |

::: mermaid
sequenceDiagram
    actor Client
    participant Portal
    participant Platform
    Client->>Portal: Choose company, Buy/Sell, quantity, Review
    Portal->>Portal: Check quantity, cash (at quote), shares held
    Client->>Portal: Confirm
    Portal->>Platform: Place order
    Platform->>Platform: New price, then check cash / shares
    Platform->>Platform: Move cash and shares, record trade, issue invoice
    Platform-->>Client: Confirmation and invoice messages
:::
### P-002 — Add funds

**Goal:** the client adds cash to the wallet.

1. The client chooses bank transfer or debit card and enters the amount and details. The portal checks them.
2. The platform checks them again and records the deposit as **Pending**. It keeps only the method and the last 4 digits.
3. After a short simulated processing time (bank transfer about 1.5 s, card about 0.8 s), the wallet is credited and the deposit becomes **Success**.
4. The client receives "Funds added successfully" with the new balance.

**Exceptions:**
- Funding never fails once the details are valid. There is no declined path.
- If the platform stops during processing, the deposit stays Pending forever (section 11).

### P-003 — Register a new client

1. The visitor enters name, email, phone and password, and the details are checked.
2. Registration is refused if the email is already registered.
3. The account (KYC Unverified) and a $0 wallet are created together.
4. A welcome message is sent.
5. The client is signed in immediately, with no email verification, and taken to the dashboard.

## 11. Known issues and risks (not requirements)

Observations from the code analysis, written as business impact. They need product and engineering decisions.

**High impact**

| ID | Issue | Business impact | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-WEB-002 | Trade and funding confirmations are sent **after** the operation has been saved, separately | If sending fails, the client sees an error for a trade or deposit that succeeded, and may repeat it | Open |
| D-WEB-004 / D-API-005 | No platform-level protection against duplicate orders or deposits | Retries, or API callers, can create duplicate trades or deposits | Open |
| D-API-001 | Trading, funding and registration rules are maintained twice (portal and API) | Changes must be made twice. The channels can drift apart (they already differ in message wording). | Open |

**Medium impact**

| ID | Issue | Business impact | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-WEB-003 | A deposit interrupted during processing stays Pending forever. Nothing reconciles it. | Client's money is "lost" in Pending, with no recovery process | Open |
| D-WEB-001 / D-API-002 | Logout doesn't invalidate the session server-side; the API has no logout | A leaked session or token works for up to 7 days | Open |
| D-WEB-007 | No limit on failed login attempts | Password guessing is possible | Open |
| D-DB-001 | Money is stored as floating-point numbers | Cent-level rounding drift over time | Open |

| D-LIB-001 | Invoice numbers use only 8 characters of the trade reference | A rare collision makes the trade fail | Open |
| D-DOM-001 | Statuses exist that are never used (Failed trade, Failed deposit, KYC Pending/Verified) | Failed operations leave no audit trail. Screens imply states that can't happen. | Open |

| ID | Issue | Business impact | Decision |
