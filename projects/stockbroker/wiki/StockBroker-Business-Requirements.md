## 2. Executive summary

StockBroker is a **simulated retail stock-broking platform**. A client:
1. registers
2. funds a cash wallet by (simulated) bank transfer or debit card
3. buys and sells whole shares in 41 listed US companies at simulated, slightly fluctuating prices
4. receives a PDF invoice and inbox notifications for every trade

No real money, market data or payment network is involved, and the product says so on every money-related screen and document.

The platform is reached through two **channels**: a self-service **web portal**, and a **public REST API** for external client applications. The API offers most of the portal's operations, but not all of them.

**Headline risks** (section 11):
- A failure after a trade has already gone through can show the client an error, which invites a duplicate trade.
- Only the portal's button prevents duplicate orders. The platform itself doesn't detect them, and API callers have no protection at all.
- The trading rules are maintained twice, once per channel.

**Key open decisions** (section 12):
- price tolerance between the quote and the execution price
- whether the illustrative brokerage fee should be charged
- the KYC process
- who the public API is for
